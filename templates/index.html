About EcoWatch Platform

EcoWatch is a municipal waste management system designed specifically for the KwaDlangezwa community.

• Residents: Report illegal dumping & waste issues with photo proof.
• Administrators: Manage reports and dispatch cleanup crews.
• Cleanup Crew: Track assigned tasks and mark them resolved.
